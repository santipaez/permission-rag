# Compensation bands by level

Bands are reviewed twice a year against the local market. Each band has a floor,
a midpoint and a ceiling. Offers below the floor require written approval from
the people director.

## Structure

Level one sits in the entry band. Level two is the professional band and is
where most engineers spend their first three years. Level three and above are
appointed, not applied for.

## Progression

Progression is driven by demonstrated scope, not by tenure. The two questions
asked in every calibration are: what did this person own that nobody else could
have owned, and what did they change about how the team works.

## Confidentiality

Band data is confidential. Managers receive the bands for their own team only,
and sharing a band outside that scope is a disciplinary matter.
