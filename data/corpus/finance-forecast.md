# Quarterly revenue forecast

The forecast closes on the tenth business day of the quarter. Finance publishes a
committed number and a weighted pipeline number. Only the committed number goes
to the board.

## Assumptions

Recurring services grow at four percent per quarter. Project revenue is booked
when the milestone is accepted by the client, not when the invoice is issued.
Any deal larger than two hundred thousand dollars is listed individually in the
appendix, because averages hide the concentration risk.

## Variance

A variance above eight percent against the committed number requires a written
explanation to the board finance committee. The explanation names the specific
deals that moved and whether the movement was timing or scope.

## Restricted

This document contains forward-looking revenue and client concentration. It is
not shared with delivery teams, and the appendix is available only to the
finance director and the chief executive.
